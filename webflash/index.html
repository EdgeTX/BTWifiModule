BTWifiModule

FrSky Bluetooth emulator for the ESP32.

Requires a Chromium-based browser (Chrome/Edge) over HTTPS or localhost, and the board connected over USB. Press RESET on the ESP32-C3 SuperMini after flashing (its native USB does not auto-reset); the CP2102 boards reset automatically.

The ESP32-S3 SuperMini, the Pico boards and the RadioMaster Wireless Module are reference builds kept for community testing: they are not hardware-validated by this project. The Pico boards differ in the radio UART pins — if one does not work, try the other.
